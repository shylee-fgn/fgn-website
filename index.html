About Us

A lean team built to deliver

FGN is a Sydney-based commercial fit-out contractor led by a strong core team that pairs hands-on trade expertise with commercial project controls. We deliver projects of all values, shapes and sizes, on time, on budget and in alignment with client expectations.

Project stakeholders deal directly with the team running the job: no layers of management, one line of communication. That means faster decisions, tighter cost control and a high quality build that reflects the design intent.
