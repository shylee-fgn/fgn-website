Company Background

FGN is a Sydney-based commercial fit-out contractor led by a small core team that pairs hands-on trade expertise with commercial project controls. Project stakeholders deal directly with the team running the job, with no layers of management and a single line of communication, which supports faster decisions, tighter cost control, and a build quality that reflects the original design intent.

The company is run by two principals: Elliot Terry-Fagan, Managing Director, with 10+ years in carpentry, building and site supervision on high-end residential and commercial projects; and Will Desborough, Construction Manager, with 7+ years in quantity surveying, project engineering and project management on commercial fit-outs and new builds. Combined, the principals have 17 years of industry experience.

Services

  • Commercial fit-outs: office, retail, hospitality and wellness fit-out delivery, coordination and management
  • Base building make-good: tenancy reinstatement, spec suites, base building upgrades
  • Joinery and high-end finishes: bespoke joinery, detailing and premium finishes
  • Scheduling and procurement: long-lead items, sampling, material sourcing and trade packaging
  • Site management and subcontractor coordination: HSEQ, stakeholder management, risk management, sequencing, site supervision
  • QS and contract administration: budgeting, value engineering, authority approvals, transparent cost reporting
